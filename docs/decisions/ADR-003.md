# ADR-003: Economics — LLM Cost vs. Trading PnL

**Status:** Draft

**Date:** 2026-10-23

---

## Context

At USD 1-2 per trade, the daily LLM API cost of running an autonomous trading agent may be comparable to or greater than the expected daily trading PnL. The owner must understand this before going live, so that the live micro phase is correctly understood as a validation phase, not a profit phase.

---

## LLM Cost Estimate

Model pricing used (as of October 2025 — verify current pricing at console.anthropic.com):
- Claude Haiku 4.5: $0.80 / MTok input, $4.00 / MTok output
- Claude Sonnet 4.6: $3.00 / MTok input, $15.00 / MTok output

### Per-call cost estimates

| Call type | Model | Input tokens | Output tokens | Cost per call |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Screening cycle | Haiku 4.5 | ~1,000 | ~100 | ~$0.0008 |
| Decision | Sonnet 4.6 | ~4,000 | ~500 | ~$0.019 |
| Post-trade review | Sonnet 4.6 | ~3,000 | ~500 | ~$0.017 |

### Daily cost projection

Assumed schedule: 6 screening cycles/hour, 1 decision per 4 cycles, 1 trade per 3 decisions.

| Component | Volume/day | Unit cost | Daily cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screening | 6 × 24 = 144 cycles | $0.0008 | ~$0.115 |
| Decisions | 144 / 4 = 36 decisions | $0.019 | ~$0.684 |
| Post-trade reviews | 36 / 3 = 12 reviews | $0.017 | ~$0.204 |
| **Total (no caching)** | | | **~$1.00–$1.50/day** |
| **With 50% prompt caching** | (static system prompts cached) | | **~$0.50–$0.75/day** |

**Hard budget cap in `config/risk.yaml`:** `total_llm_budget_usd_per_day = 5.00`

When this cap is reached, the system stops calling LLMs for the rest of the UTC day. All decisions become `NO_TRADE`. This is a hard stop enforced by the Risk Gate.

---

## Trading PnL Estimate (Maximum Possible)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| max_loss_per_trade_usd | $1.00 |
| max_trades_per_day | 6 |
| Assumed R:R | 1.5 (minimum from config) |
| Assumed win rate | 50% (optimistic for a new system) |

Expected value per trade = 0.50 × ($1.00 × 1.5) − 0.50 × $1.00 = +$0.25

| Scenario | Trades/day | Expected daily PnL |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum (6 trades) | 6 | **$1.50/day** |
| Realistic (3 trades) | 3 | **$0.75/day** |
| Conservative (1 trade) | 1 | **$0.25/day** |

---

## Net Economics Summary

| Scenario | Trading PnL | LLM cost | Net daily result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Optimistic (6 trades, caching) | +$1.50 | -$0.50 | **+$1.00** |
| Realistic (3 trades, no caching) | +$0.75 | -$1.25 | **-$0.50** |
| Conservative (1 trade, no caching) | +$0.25 | -$1.25 | **-$1.00** |

**In most realistic scenarios, the net daily P&L will be negative during the live micro phase.** This is expected and acceptable.

---

## Decision

**The live micro phase is a validation phase, not a profit phase.**

The goal is to prove:
1. The system makes correct decisions (good decision quality as judged by post-trade review)
2. Risk discipline holds (zero cap breaches, zero unprotected positions)
3. Trading expectancy (R-multiple, excluding LLM cost) is positive or at least not clearly negative
4. The repeat-mistake rate trends toward zero over time

Reports must always show:
- **Trading PnL** (gross, before LLM and data costs) — this measures the trading system's quality
- **LLM + data cost** (separate line item) — this measures the system's operating cost
- **Net P&L** (trading PnL minus costs) — for completeness only

The owner decides scaling based on **trading expectancy (R-multiple)**, not on the net number that the LLM bill dominates at micro scale.

---

## Break-even Scale

At ~10x scale ($10–20 per trade), the same LLM cost is roughly 10x smaller relative to trading PnL:
- Trading PnL (optimistic): ~$15/day
- LLM cost: ~$1.00–$1.50/day (does not scale with trade size)
- Net P&L: ~$13.50–$14.00/day

This illustrates that the economics become significantly better as trade size scales, assuming trading expectancy remains positive.

---

## Consequences

- Phase 10 validation report must include a separate LLM cost column in all trade tables.
- The Telegram daily summary must show trading PnL and LLM cost as separate numbers.
- The decision to scale (raise the per-trade cap) must be based on sustained positive R-multiple evidence, reviewed in Phase 12.
- The owner should not interpret a negative net daily P&L during micro phase as a system failure — it is the expected operating cost of the learning and validation process.

---

## Owner Confirmation Required

The owner must confirm they understand and accept:
1. Net daily P&L will likely be negative during the live micro phase.
2. The $5.00/day LLM hard cap means the system will stop making decisions when the cap is hit.
3. The system's value in Phase 11 is validated by trading expectancy (R), not by net P&L.
